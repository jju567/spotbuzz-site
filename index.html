Key Features

Voice & Spoken Reminders (TTS)

Say “Walmart buy milk” to search within 50 km via OpenStreetMap. On arrival, SpotBuzz can also read reminders and places aloud offline (with headphones-only mode).

Live Radar & Walk Radar

Real-time radar scope with distance, bearing, and True North compass calibration. Walk Radar continues lightweight proximity alerts even with the screen off.

Google Maps & File Import

Share places directly from Google Maps or import travel lists from KML, GeoJSON, CSV, and JSON files into organized folders.

Battery-Friendly Geofencing

Uses Android’s hardware-backed Geofencing API with a 2 km rolling anchor for your nearest 50 targets — no constant background GPS polling.

100% Private & Offline

Your places, reminders, and logs stay on your device. No account required, no analytics SDKs, and cloud/ADB backup is disabled.

Built for Travelers (17 Languages)

Full interface and voice search support in 17 languages including English, Finnish, Swedish, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, and Korean.
